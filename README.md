### Convoia AI — Multi-LLM SaaS Platform with Agentic System

<p>
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Node.js-339933?style=flat-square&logo=nodedotjs&logoColor=white">
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/TypeScript-3178C6?style=flat-square&logo=typescript&logoColor=white">
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/React-61DAFB?style=flat-square&logo=react&logoColor=black">

  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Prisma-2D3748?style=flat-square&logo=prisma&logoColor=white">
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Stripe-635BFF?style=flat-square&logo=stripe&logoColor=white">
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Clerk-6C47FF?style=flat-square&logo=clerk&logoColor=white">
</p>

Built and shipped solo in under 6 months. Production SaaS supporting **6 AI providers across 16 models** with real-time model switching and agent mode.

- **12 specialized agents** — code generation, document analysis, web research, SQL querying, summarization, creative writing, data extraction, legal drafting — routed through a custom agent router with tool use and chain-of-thought reasoning
- **RAG pipeline** with vector search, semantic chunking, and retrieval scoring over user-uploaded documents
- **Token wallet billing** with org-level budget enforcement, pre-query balance validation, Stripe Checkout, and webhook-based crediting across a 4-tier RBAC hierarchy
- **Image generation** via DALL·E 3 and Gemini Imagen on a fixed-cost token model
- Resolved production bugs including Stripe webhook misfires, cross-account session leakage, and token overspend race conditions

Founder. Domain-specific legal AI making Indian legal knowledge accessible through conversational AI, document analysis, and case research automation.

- **RAG grounded in Indian legal corpora** — IPC, CrPC, Constitution of India, landmark case law — with semantic retrieval, reranking, and citation-aware generation
- Specialized agents for document drafting, FIR guidance, rights awareness, and case summarization, each with guardrails for sensitive legal scenarios
- **Fine-tuned on a curated legal QA dataset → ~42% reduction in hallucination** on jurisdiction-specific queries versus a zero-shot baseline
- Multi-tenant architecture with RBAC, encrypted document storage, and audit logging
